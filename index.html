What's inside

Everyone knows D-Day and Pearl Harbor. The war was decided in a hundred other places.

Most books repeat the same five famous battles. This one goes to the convoys, the code rooms, the factories and the islands nobody remembers, and shows how each one moved the war.

Every chapter opens with a one-page map and closes with a short "why it mattered", so you never lose the thread.

  • The decisions: the choices by generals and leaders that changed everything, and the ones that nearly did.
  • The machines: the tanks, aircraft and ships, and the real reasons some won and some failed.
  • The people: first-hand stories of soldiers, pilots, sailors and civilians.
  • The myths: the popular "facts" about WW2 that do not hold up, and what really happened.
