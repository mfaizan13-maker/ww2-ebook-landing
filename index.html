No. The stories from the videos are expanded with the numbers, timelines and decision points, and many operations were never covered on the channel at all: Mincemeat, the Ghost Army, Castle Itter, St Nazaire and more.

Is the print big enough to read comfortably?

Yes. Every book is set in a deliberately large, clean type size, so it is easy on the eyes on a tablet or printed out.

Are the maps real geographic maps?

They are schematics, clearly marked "not to scale". Distances are compressed so the shape of each battle fits on one page and is easy to follow.

Is Patton's War just praise for Patton?

No. It is a balanced account. The Sicily slapping scandal, the Argentan controversy and the Hammelburg raid are all covered on the record, from both sides.

Yes. The War in Maps & Photos was made to be browsed and gifted, and the full library is the best fit for a history-loving father or grandfather.
